About Alpha Tracker: what it is and how to try it

Alpha Tracker records evidence at the moment money is spent, compares claims with each other, and shows stock losses as they happen. It has two modules sharing one ledger and one tamper-evident audit trail.

Expense proof: line items, receipt photo, GPS, signed declaration, vendor confirmation, owner approval, bank match, and a six-level evidence ladder.
Inventory: batches with expiry, loss calculator, request-and-approve write-offs, blind stock counts and reconciliation.

Demo data is already loaded for two fictional businesses: Adamu Fresh Mart (supermarket, Abuja) and Eze Care Pharmacy (Lekki). Everyone signs in with their own PIN. Owner 0000, then David 1111, Paul 2222, Amaka 3333, John 4444 (pharmacy: Chidi 1111, Bisi 2222, Tunde 3333, Sade 4444).
